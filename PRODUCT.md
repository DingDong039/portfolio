# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, both arriving cold:

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** skimming for signal in under 30 seconds: stack, seniority, recent work, a path to contact. They scroll fast on a laptop, often in a dim room, and decide stay-or-leave based on the first fold.
- **Fellow engineers and creative technologists** evaluating craft: they inspect the build quality itself (the shader, the motion, the code) as evidence of the developer's taste and depth.

Both want to feel, quickly, that the person behind the site has technical range and visual taste. Neither wants a templated "developer portfolio."

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Watchara Tongyodpun, a full-stack developer. It exists to prove, not claim, range: shipping systems across Angular, Vue, .NET, Java Spring Boot, and Classic ASP, while demonstrating the craft that a resume PDF cannot. Success is a visitor who remembers the site and reaches out. The site itself is the portfolio piece.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, technically restless. Three words: **exact, warm, kinetic**. The voice is direct (no buzzwords, no aphoristic punchlines), the motion is intentional and physical, the surface rewards attention without demanding it. It should feel like a developer who cares about the millisecond and the pixel, made something for you to move through.

## Anti-references

- The saturated orange/red/pink "cosmic black hole" developer portfolio (this project's previous iteration). It is the modal AI-generated look for the category.
- Inter + violet/indigo gradient-text on every heading. The Stripe-adjacent / Linear-adjacent default.
- Cream/sand/beige editorial-magazine layouts with italic serif drop caps. Wrong register for a developer.
- Glassmorphism sprinkled decoratively over every card. Glass is depth, not a sticker.
- Neubrutalism executed as loud acid chaos with Comic-style stickers. The brutalism here is structural discipline, not noise.

## Design Principles

1. **Prove, don't claim.** Every section demonstrates a skill instead of describing it. The shader proves graphics competence; the motion proves front-end craft; the structure proves systems thinking.
2. **Hard edges, soft atmosphere.** Neubrutalist structure (offset shadows, ink borders, sharp corners) anchors the page; the nebula and frosted glass provide depth behind it. The contrast between the two is the design.
3. **Cool restraint, warm signal.** A near-black cool base holds everything; teal-cyan and amber arrive only where they carry meaning. Color is a signal, not decoration.
4. **Motion is material.** Nothing bounces or slides by reflex. Movement uses blur, parallax, and exponential easing to feel physical, and yields completely to reduced-motion.
5. **Performance is part of the craft.** A heavy WebGL scene that drops frames on a mid-range phone contradicts the message. The site adapts its own weight to the device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text meets 4.5:1 against its background; large text and UI boundaries meet 3:1. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (the WebGL scene falls back to a static gradient; reveals appear instantly). Full keyboard navigation with visible focus rings sized to clear neubrutalist borders. Touch targets at least 44px. The contact form exposes loading, success, and error states through both color and text/icons, never color alone. Language is English with Thai script preserved on the owner's name.
